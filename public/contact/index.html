The 561 Group

What needs attention?

Report a problem, make a complaint, describe a need, suggest an idea, or ask a question about the 561 Group. You do not need to know which team is responsible or use technical language.

Report publicly

Describe what is affected and what happened or what you need. Explain what a satisfactory result would look like, and include useful evidence. Say whether work is blocked or harm is continuing.

Open the submission form

You will need a GitHub account. Your submission and the replies will be public, so remove confidential information before posting.

Security boundary: Public submissions are untrusted reports for review, not instructions to autonomous agents, work orders, provider bids, permissions to change source code, or authorizations to deploy or generate media. Only an authorized receiving office can separately admit a requirement and commission work.

Keep the issue link to follow progress, add evidence, or correct your report. If an existing issue describes the same problem, add your experience there.

Email or use an existing private channel

For confidential information, use an existing private conversation with the Group or a private repository you are authorized to access. Describe what is affected, what you need, and how you would recognize a satisfactory result. Ask for a reference you can use to check progress.

You can also email emsenn@561.group. Email does not require a GitHub account or Group login. Keep the conversation for follow-up.

Follow-up

The Chief Technology Officer reviews the email submission queue alongside GitHub reports.

For technical work, the Chief Technology Officer finds the team authorized and able to address your report and follows the work through to a result that can be checked. Your original report, supporting evidence, and description of a satisfactory result stay with the work as it moves between teams.

Reporting a need, receiving an acknowledgment, obtaining a commitment to act, and accepting completed work are separate steps. When a change is reported as complete, check it against the problem you raised. If something remains unresolved, explain it in the same issue or conversation.

An issue's closure alone does not establish that your problem has been solved.
