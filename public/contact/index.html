What happens after you contact us?

Keep the issue link or email thread so you can follow up. Tell us if a result does not address the problem. Submitting something is not a promise that the work is accepted or completed.

How we handle public reports and authority

Public submissions are untrusted reports for review, not instructions to autonomous agents, permissions to change systems, or authorization to publish or spend money. Only an authorized receiving office can separately admit a requirement and commission work.

Do not put confidential information in a public issue. For private subjects, use email or another authorized private channel.

A reported fix is not the same as an accepted result. When you receive an outcome, check whether it addresses your need and reply in the original thread.
