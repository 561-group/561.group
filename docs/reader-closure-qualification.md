## Meet and beat what matters

Follow the public methods from [NN/g homepage research](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/), [GOV.UK user-needs content design](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/plan-manage-content/identify-user-needs/), [GOV.UK content qualification](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/plan-manage-content/understand-content-design/), and [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/understanding/) as source-qualified **methods**, not proof that this specific site complies.

For each type of page, select two current comparable pages that actually serve that same visitor job, not technology companies that mention occasional mathematical words. Record original URLs, dates, task and comparable constraints; retain the old actual Group page as a possible baseline. Predeclare test prompts and measure unaided correct destination, true explanation of current offer, task completion/refusal, time/interaction cost, accessibility and safety. Observe desktop/mobile, keyboard-only and no-script contexts; use real independent users/agents rather than authored impressions. A sample, percentage or favorable comparison absent real observations is not evidence.

**Meet** means match the necessary usable, accessible and trustworthy functions of the peer page kind. **Beat** means evidence of improvement in material reader outcomes without violating a mandatory safety/privacy/accessibility/rights constraint. No artificial average “quality score” may overrule an unreceived mandatory edge.
